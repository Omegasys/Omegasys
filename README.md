Hello, I’m Omega 👋

I have relatively little formal programming experience, but I have a lot of ideas that I’d love to explore, build, and share.

My goal is, in a manner of speaking, to “fill the potholes” instead of simply “gazing at the stars.”

A lot of technology is focused on imagining what the future could look like. I’m interested in looking at the places where technology could be more useful, accessible, secure, private, modular, or simply work a little better—and then trying to build something that addresses those gaps.

I’m a vibe coder. I use AI as a tool to help turn ideas into working projects while learning along the way. I know there is an ongoing debate around AI-assisted and AI-generated code, and I understand the concerns that come with it.

My approach is simple:

Make the ideas public.

I want my projects to be open for people to inspect, experiment with, improve, rewrite, criticize, fork, or take in directions I never considered. There may be parts of the code that I don’t fully understand yet, but that’s part of the reason I publish it.

Maybe someone who understands the code better than I do will see something worth developing.

Maybe someone will find a problem I missed.

Maybe someone will take an idea and turn it into something far better than I could have made on my own.

And that’s okay.

The goal isn’t for me to write everything perfectly. The goal is to put ideas into the world where they have a chance to become something more.

🌱 Why Open Source?

I believe an idea becomes more interesting when other people can build on it.

If you find something here that interests you, fork it, experiment with it, improve it, or take the idea somewhere completely different.

You don’t have to agree with how I approached something. In fact, discovering better approaches is one of the reasons I put these projects out in the open.

I’m learning as I go, and hopefully the projects can evolve along with me.

⸻

🧪 Project Status

Not every repository here should be expected to be production-ready.

I use the following general status labels to help communicate where a project currently stands:

* 🧪 Experimental — An idea or early experiment.
* 🚧 Early Development — The project is actively being built but may change considerably.
* 🔨 Active Development — The project has a working foundation and is being actively expanded.
* 🟢 Usable — The project is functional enough for practical use, although improvements may still be underway.
* 📦 Stable — The project has reached a relatively mature state.
* 💤 Archived — Development has been paused or discontinued.

A project can move between these stages as it evolves.

💡 Project Philosophy

Ideas don’t have to start perfect. They just have to start.

I would rather put an imperfect idea into the world where someone can improve it than keep it hidden because I don’t yet have the skills to build it perfectly.

Some of my projects may start as little more than an idea, a specification, a prototype, or an experiment.

That’s intentional.

The first version doesn’t have to be the final version.

Someone else might discover a better architecture. Someone might find a security problem. Someone might completely rethink the original concept.

Those aren’t failures.

That’s how open source is supposed to work.

🛣️ Fill the Potholes

The technology world is full of people looking toward the next big thing.

I want to spend some of my time looking down at the road.

There are potholes everywhere.

Some are tiny inconveniences. Some are problems people have simply gotten used to. Others may be problems that require completely new approaches.

I want to experiment with ideas that might help fill some of those potholes.

Maybe the solution already exists.

Maybe it doesn’t.

Maybe my idea is wrong.

Maybe someone else will take the idea and build the right solution.

Any of those outcomes can be useful.
